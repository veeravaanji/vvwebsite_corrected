ABOUT OUR ACADEMY

Preserving Tamil Martial Heritage

Veera Vaanji Martial Arts Academy is dedicated to preserving and promoting the ancient Tamil martial art Silambam through professional training, discipline and traditional values.

Our academy provides quality training for children, students, women and adults. We focus on self-defence, fitness, confidence, leadership and championship-level coaching while preserving our rich Tamil culture.

WHO WE ARE

Our Mission, Vision & Values

We are committed to preserving the rich heritage of Tamil martial arts while building disciplined, confident and responsible individuals.

Our Mission

To preserve and promote the ancient Tamil martial art of Silambam by providing quality training that develops discipline, confidence and leadership among students.

தமிழர்களின் பாரம்பரிய வீரக்கலையான சிலம்பத்தை அடுத்த தலைமுறைக்கு பாதுகாத்து கொண்டு செல்லுதல்.

Our Vision

To become one of India's leading martial arts academies by producing disciplined athletes, skilled instructors and national-level champions.

உலகளவில் சிலம்பக் கலையை பரப்பி சிறந்த வீரர்களை உருவாக்குவது.
